# WatchParty Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

WatchParty is a browser extension that lets you watch videos together with
friends, with camera video and chat in a side panel.

## What we collect

Nothing. WatchParty has no servers of its own, no accounts, no analytics and
no trackers. The developer never receives, stores or sells any of your data.

## How your data moves

- **Video, audio and chat** travel directly between you and the people in
  your room using encrypted peer-to-peer WebRTC connections (DTLS-SRTP).
  They are never stored anywhere and never pass through a server we run.
- **Signaling**: to connect peers to each other, the extension exchanges a
  short connection handshake through the public PeerJS cloud
  (peerjs.com), or through your own server if you configure one in
  Settings. Only connection metadata is exchanged — no video, audio or
  message content.
- **Your profile** (display name, avatar, name color, badge), your device
  choices and your settings are stored locally in your browser using the
  extension's storage. They are shared only with the people in the room you
  join, and leave your machine no other way.

## Camera and microphone

The camera and microphone are used only to show and play you to the people
in your room, only after you grant permission, and never recorded. You can
watch with the camera off; an avatar is shown instead.

## Permissions

- **Access to websites** is used to draw the WatchParty side panel on the
  page you are watching and, if you enable playback sync, to play, pause and
  seek that page's video. Page content is not read or collected.
- **Storage** keeps your profile and settings on your device.
